I am a theoretical and computational physicist and a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute of Physics, Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz, working with Friederike Schmid. I study how molecular interactions and active, energy-consuming processes control self-organization in soft and biological matter, from biomolecular condensates and active materials to block copolymers and DNA in nanopores.

My approach connects three levels of description: particle-based simulations (coarse-grained molecular, Langevin and Brownian dynamics, lattice Boltzmann, kinetic Monte Carlo), polymer field theory (self-consistent field theory and particle–field models), and continuum phase-field dynamics. Since 2022, I have released the codes and data behind my papers openly on [GitHub](https://github.com/leqiao).

Before Mainz, I completed my PhD in Physics with Gary W. Slater at the University of Ottawa, on the theory and simulation of polymer capture and translocation through nanopores, and an MPhil in Biological Physics at the University of Manchester.
